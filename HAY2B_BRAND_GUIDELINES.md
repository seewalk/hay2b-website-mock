## Logo & Brand Identity

### Primary Logo
The Hay2B Logistics logo features:
- **Company Name**: "Hay2B" in bold, modern typography
- **Service Descriptor**: "LOGISTICS" in uppercase
- **Tagline**: "We Always Deliver" positioned below or adjacent to the company name
- **Icon Element**: Yellow/gold arrow or directional element suggesting movement and delivery

### Logo Variations

#### Full Logo (Horizontal)
- Company name + tagline
- Use for: Letterheads, business cards, marketing materials

#### Logo Mark Only
- Simplified version without tagline
- Use for: Social media avatars, small applications

#### Monochrome Version
- Black and white version for single-color applications
- Use for: Faxes, stamps, single-color printing

### Logo Clear Space
Maintain a clear space around the logo equal to the height of the "H" in "Hay2B" on all sides.

### Minimum Size
- Digital: 120px wide
- Print: 1 inch (25mm) wide

### Logo Don'ts
❌ Do not stretch or distort the logo
❌ Do not change logo colors
❌ Do not add effects (shadows, gradients, etc.)
❌ Do not place on busy backgrounds
❌ Do not rotate the logo
❌ Do not separate logo elements

## Color Palette

### Primary Colors

#### Yellow/Gold (Brand Primary)
- **HEX**: `#FFD700` / `#FDB913`
- **RGB**: 253, 185, 19
- **CMYK**: 0, 27, 92, 1
- **Usage**: Primary brand color, accents, highlights, calls-to-action
- **Meaning**: Energy, optimism, reliability, attention

#### Black (Brand Secondary)
- **HEX**: `#000000` / `#1A1A1A`
- **RGB**: 0, 0, 0
- **CMYK**: 75, 68, 67, 90
- **Usage**: Text, headings, logo, professional applications
- **Meaning**: Professionalism, sophistication, authority

#### White
- **HEX**: `#FFFFFF`
- **RGB**: 255, 255, 255
- **CMYK**: 0, 0, 0, 0
- **Usage**: Backgrounds, text on dark backgrounds, spacing

### Secondary Colors

#### Gray (Supporting)
- **HEX**: `#808080` / `#666666`
- **RGB**: 128, 128, 128
- **CMYK**: 0, 0, 0, 50
- **Usage**: Body text, borders, subtle backgrounds

#### Red (Accent/Alert)
- **HEX**: `#C41E3A` / `#CC0000`
- **RGB**: 196, 30, 58
- **CMYK**: 0, 85, 70, 23
- **Usage**: Important information, alerts, urgent notices (used sparingly)

### Color Combinations

#### High Contrast (Primary)
- Yellow/Gold + Black
- Use for: Headlines, important information, CTAs

#### Professional (Secondary)
- Black + White with Yellow accents
- Use for: Business documents, presentations

#### Backgrounds
- White backgrounds with black text and yellow accents
- Light gray backgrounds for sections

## Typography

### Primary Typeface

#### Sans-Serif Font Family
**Recommended**: Arial, Helvetica, or similar modern sans-serif
- Clean, professional, highly readable
- Consistent across print and digital

### Font Hierarchy

#### Headlines (H1)
- **Font**: Arial Black / Helvetica Bold
- **Weight**: Bold/Heavy
- **Size**: 24-36pt (print) / 32-48px (digital)
- **Color**: Black or Yellow/Gold
- **Usage**: Main titles, section headers

#### Subheadings (H2-H3)
- **Font**: Arial Bold / Helvetica Bold
- **Weight**: Bold
- **Size**: 16-24pt (print) / 20-32px (digital)
- **Color**: Black
- **Usage**: Section titles, subsections

#### Body Text
- **Font**: Arial / Helvetica Regular
- **Weight**: Regular
- **Size**: 10-12pt (print) / 14-16px (digital)
- **Color**: Black or Dark Gray (#333333)
- **Line Height**: 1.5-1.7
- **Usage**: Paragraphs, descriptions, content

#### Small Text / Captions
- **Font**: Arial / Helvetica Regular
- **Weight**: Regular
- **Size**: 8-10pt (print) / 12-14px (digital)
- **Color**: Gray (#666666)
- **Usage**: Footnotes, captions, fine print

### Typography Guidelines
- Use sentence case for most content (capitalize first word only)
- Use ALL CAPS sparingly for emphasis (e.g., "LOGISTICS")
- Maintain consistent spacing between elements
- Align text left for readability (centered for titles when appropriate)

## Brand Voice & Messaging

### Brand Voice
**Professional | Reliable | Confident | Customer-Focused**

### Tone Characteristics

#### Professional
- Clear and concise language
- Industry-appropriate terminology
- Grammatically correct
- Respectful and courteous

#### Reliable
- Consistent messaging
- Factual and accurate information
- Straightforward communication
- Follow-through on promises

#### Confident
- Assertive but not aggressive
- Knowledgeable and expert
- Solution-oriented
- Proactive communication

#### Customer-Focused
- Friendly and approachable
- Responsive to needs
- Helpful and supportive
- Clear call-to-actions

### Key Messages

#### Core Message
"Hay2B Logistics - We Always Deliver"

#### Supporting Messages
- "Your trusted logistics partner"
- "Reliable transportation solutions"
- "On-time, every time"
- "Professional logistics services you can count on"
- "Delivering excellence in every shipment"

### Writing Style

#### Do:
✅ Use active voice
✅ Be clear and direct
✅ Focus on benefits to customers
✅ Include specific details (rates, times, locations)
✅ End with clear next steps

#### Don't:
❌ Use jargon without explanation
❌ Make promises you can't keep
❌ Use overly casual language
❌ Include unnecessary complexity
❌ Bury important information

## Visual Style

### Photography Style

#### Preferred Imagery
- **Logistics in Action**: Trucks, warehouses, delivery scenes
- **Professional**: Clean, well-lit, high quality
- **People**: Friendly staff, satisfied customers
- **Equipment**: Modern vehicles, organized facilities
- **Movement**: Dynamic shots showing activity and progress

#### Photo Characteristics
- Natural lighting preferred
- Sharp focus and clear subjects
- Professional composition
- Authentic (not overly staged)
- Diverse representation of people and services

### Graphic Elements

#### Arrows & Directional Elements
- Use yellow/gold arrows to indicate movement, direction, progress
- Simple, clean lines
- Points forward or upward (positive progression)

#### Icons
- Simple, line-based icons
- Consistent stroke weight
- Black or yellow/gold color
- Common logistics symbols: trucks, packages, checkmarks, clocks

#### Patterns & Textures
- Minimal use of patterns
- Subtle diagonal lines or stripes (suggesting movement)
- Yellow/gold accents on white or gray backgrounds

### Layout Principles

#### Grid System
- Use consistent grid for alignment
- Maintain generous white space
- Balance text and visuals
- Clear hierarchy of information

#### Spacing
- Consistent margins (minimum 0.5 inch / 12mm)
- Adequate padding around elements
- Group related information
- Use white space to create breathing room
