## 📐 Logo Guidelines

**Clear Space**: Equal to height of "H" in "Hay2B"  
**Minimum Size**: 120px digital / 1 inch print

**Do**:
✅ Use approved logo files  
✅ Maintain color accuracy  
✅ Keep proper clear space  
✅ Scale proportionally

**Don't**:
❌ Stretch or distort  
❌ Change colors  
❌ Add effects  
❌ Rotate or modify

---

## 📄 Document Standards

### Margins
- Minimum: 0.5 inch / 12mm all sides
- Generous white space throughout

### Color Usage
- **Documents**: Black text on white background with yellow accents
- **Headers**: Yellow/gold bars or accents
- **Highlights**: Yellow for important information

### Layout
- Left-aligned text (professional)
- Clear hierarchy
- Consistent spacing
- Professional imagery
